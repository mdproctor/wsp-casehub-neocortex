# D2 Exploration: Situational Modulation Literature Review

## Stress Modulation

11 of 14 facets amplify under stress (consistent with the broader personality-under-stress literature: stress activates maladaptive features by depleting regulatory resources).

| Facet | Direction | Magnitude | Confidence | Key Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Anhedonia | ↑ | Med-Large | STRONG | Pizzagalli 2014 (mesolimbic DA dysfunction); stress-kindling model |
| Depressivity | ↑ | Large | STRONG | Diathesis-stress model (Beck 1967); Hankin & Abramson 2001 |
| Perseveration | ↑ | Medium | STRONG | Nature Sci Reports 2021 (stress → rigid/habitual); cortisol disrupts PFC set-shifting |
| Rigid Perfectionism | ↑ (complex) | Medium | MODERATE | PMC 2022 (self-critical perfectionism amplified by stress); HPA overactivation paradox |
| Distractibility | ↑ | Medium | STRONG | PMC 2024 (stress shifts dlPFC → amygdala); chronic stress → PFC dendritic retraction |
| Separation Insecurity | ↑ | Med-Large | STRONG | Mikulincer & Shaver 2003 (hyperactivation strategy amplifies distress signals) |
| Restricted Affectivity | ↑ (numbing deepens) | Small-Med | MODERATE | Alexithymia intensified during burnout; PTSD emotional numbing as conditioned response |
| Irresponsibility | ↑ (via depletion) | Small-Med | MODERATE | Ego depletion model (Baumeister); but construct itself contested |
| Cog/Perceptual Dysregulation | ↑ | Med-Large | STRONG | Peritraumatic dissociation (Bremner); Lanius 2010 dissociative PTSD subtype |
| Eccentricity | ↑ (disorganized dim.) | Small-Med | MODERATE | Rónai 2026 (disorganized schizotypy predicts stress-reactivity in daily life) |
| Submissiveness | ↑ | Medium | MODERATE | PNAS 2025 (stress + low rank → enhanced submissiveness + amygdala changes) |
| Unusual Beliefs | ↑ | Small-Med | MODERATE | Keinan 1994/2002 (stress → magical thinking, Gulf War study) |
| Deceitfulness | Unchanged | — | WEAK | No clear amplification — deception is strategic, not reactive |
| Attention Seeking | ↑ (dysregulation) | Small-Med | WEAK | Clinical observation only, no experimental evidence |

## Flow Modulation

Two opposing effects: (1) DMN/self-consciousness suppression dampens self-referential facets, (2) absorption/reduced latent inhibition amplifies positive schizotypy features.

| Facet | Direction | Confidence | Key Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Depressivity | ↓ | STRONG | Twin study (Transl Psych 2024, flow protects against depression); RFCBT uses flow to counter rumination |
| Distractibility | ↓ strongly | STRONG | Flow = antithesis of distractibility; Ashinoff & Abu-Akel 2021 (hyperfocus criteria) |
| Anhedonia | ↓ | MODERATE | Flow IS sustained intrinsic reward; temporarily overrides anhedonic baseline via DA |
| Rigid Perfectionism | ↓ (evaluative suppressed) | MODERATE | 2×2 model: ECP (≈PID-5) inhibits flow; flow suppresses self-criticism via transient hypofrontality |
| Cog/Perceptual Dysregulation | ↑ (absorption overlap) | MODERATE | JAEX 2022 ("In the Zone or Disconnected" — flow components correlate with dissociation) |
| Eccentricity | ↑ (creative flow) | MODERATE | Nelson & Rawlings 2010 (positive schizotypy = strongest predictor of creative experience) |
| Perseveration | ↓ (pathological) | WEAK | Inferred from flow ≠ perseveration (voluntary vs involuntary engagement) |
| Separation Insecurity | ↓ | WEAK | Inferred from self-consciousness loss |
| Submissiveness | ↓ | WEAK | Inferred from SDT autonomy boost |
| Restricted Affectivity | Unclear | NONE | No research found |
| Irresponsibility | Unclear | NONE | No research found |
| Unusual Beliefs | ↓/complex | WEAK | Reduced uncertainty → less magical thinking, but absorption component may ↑ |
| Deceitfulness | ↓ (theoretical) | NONE | Authenticity in flow; theoretical only |
| Attention Seeking | ↓ | WEAK | Self-consciousness loss suppresses social self-presentation |

## Social Context Modulation

### Collaborative

| Facet | Direction | Confidence | Key Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Depressivity | ↑ | STRONG | Co-rumination with partners; social comparison rumination (Flett 2024) |
| Rigid Perfectionism | ↑ | STRONG | Socially prescribed perfectionism (perceived pressure from others' expectations) |
| Irresponsibility | ↓ | MODERATE | Social accountability improves conscientiousness-linked performance |
| Submissiveness | ↓ | MODERATE | Safety system activation (Gilbert) reduces need for submission |
| Separation Insecurity | ↓ | MODERATE | Supportive contexts activate safety system |
| Restricted Affectivity | ↑ | MODERATE | Collaborative interactions demand emotional expression/communication |
| Anhedonia | ↑ | MODERATE | Social rewards blunted in anhedonics |
| Attention Seeking | ↑ | MODERATE | Audience available |
| Perseveration | ↑ | MODERATE | Co-rumination amplifies perseverative cognition (Nature 2025) |

### Competitive

| Facet | Direction | Confidence | Key Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Depressivity | ↑ | STRONG | Upward social comparison → negative self-evaluation (McCarthy & Morina 2020 meta) |
| Rigid Perfectionism | ↑ | STRONG | Evaluative contexts = primary trigger (Curran & Hill 2019 meta) |
| Separation Insecurity | ↑ | STRONG | Competition activates dominance-status striving (Gasiorowska 2026 N=4,456) |
| Deceitfulness | ↑ | STRONG | Competition = primary trigger for Machiavellian strategy (Bereczkei 2015) |
| Submissiveness | ↑ | MODERATE | Competitive contexts where losing has consequences trigger deferral |
| Eccentricity | ↓ | MODERATE | Conformity pressure suppresses eccentric expression (Asch 1951) |
| Attention Seeking | ↑ | MODERATE | Audience + opportunity for dramatic differentiation |

### Hierarchical

| Facet | Direction | Confidence | Key Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Submissiveness | ↑ | STRONG | THE primary trigger — dominance elicits submission (Tiedens & Fragale 2003) |
| Separation Insecurity | ↑ | STRONG | Social rank salience activates rank processing (Gilbert social rank theory) |
| Irresponsibility | ↓ | MODERATE | Authority enforcement activates compliance |
| Eccentricity | ↓ | MODERATE | Conformity pressure from authority |
| Deceitfulness | ↑ | MODERATE | Power differential enables strategic manipulation (Stackelberg) |
| Depressivity | ↑ | MODERATE | Lower perceived rank → depression |
| Cog/Perceptual Dysregulation | ↑ | MODERATE | Social stress/evaluation → dissociation (SAD patients) |
| Rigid Perfectionism | ↑ | MODERATE | Authority-imposed standards activate socially prescribed perfectionism |
